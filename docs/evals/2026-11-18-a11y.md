# WCAG 2.2 AA pass (I6), 2026-11-18

Automated in `e2e/wcag22.spec.ts` (runs in CI with the rest of the suite):

- **axe-core, WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A+AA rules** (includes 2.5.8 target size) on 34 screens plus every step and
  feedback sheet of three lessons that use the new exercise types, in **light and dark**: 0 violations.
- **Keyboard only:** a whole lesson (teach cards, choices, typing, word tiles, match pairs, speak skip)
  completed without a pointer; the right answer is reachable by Tab.
- **200% text size at 375px:** no horizontal scrolling and no clipped content on 10 main screens. The
  prep-pack carousel is a deliberate horizontal scroller.
- **prefers-reduced-motion:** every animation and transition drops to ≤ 0.01 ms.

Fixed in this pass:
- 2.5.3 Label in Name: scene lines, family-tree nodes and the story play-all button had accessible names
  that didn't contain their visible text. They now use visible text plus visually hidden context.
- 1.4.3 Contrast: amber text on amber-soft (placement and comeback pills, Respect meter) darkened in light
  mode; faded scene placeholders now full contrast; Nastaliq in "both" mode no longer drawn at 80% opacity;
  destructive links use the deep red token, not the on-red-button ink.
- 1.4.10 Reflow: settings segmented controls and the boss intro card wrap; bottom-nav labels can break;
  decorative hero emoji can no longer widen the page.
- 2.4.7 / 2.4.13 Focus: one high-contrast focus ring (3 px, deep teal) on every focusable element.

Screen reader notes (manual review of the accessibility tree): every exercise heading is an h2; answer
groups are radiogroups with aria-checked; feedback is announced through role=status / aria-live=assertive;
conversations are an aria-live=polite log; Urdu text carries lang="ur" dir="rtl". Not yet tested with real
VoiceOver/TalkBack on devices: do that before store submission (docs/RELEASE.md).
