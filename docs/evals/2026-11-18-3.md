  (11 of 11). This is the founder's own case.

## Problems found and fixed in this pass
1. **Teach walls.** Long lessons showed up to 9 teach cards before any practice (poetry: 8 couplet
   lines in a row). Fix: lessons with 5+ phrase cards now interleave (teach ~3, practise those, teach
   the next ~3). Pure engine function `interleave()` with tests.
2. "Knock on the door" was the start button for every conversation (the news, the bazaar, a
   mushaira). Now "Start the conversation".
3. Insights said "1 answers so far" and "0 minutes" after real practice. Fixed plural and shows "<1".
