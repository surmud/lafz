# Audio audit — 2026-11-16

Tool: `scripts/audio_audit.py` round-trips normal-speed MMS clips through Whisper
(MLX `whisper-large-v3-turbo`, language ur) and scores similarity on normalised Urdu text
(1 − edit distance / length). Full results: `docs/audio-audit-v1.json` (150-clip sample, voice v1)
and `docs/audio-audit.json` (40-clip subset, voice v2).

## Findings (v1, 150 clips)
- Sentences of 3+ words: median similarity **0.87**. Only 3 of 90 were below 0.5.
- 1-word phrases: median **0.33**, 28 of 47 below 0.5. Isolated words are hard for Whisper too, so this
  overstates how bad they sound, but MMS also clips and mumbles them.
- Specific mispronunciation: "السلام علیکم" comes back as "تلسلام…" (the Arabic article is voiced oddly).

## Change: voice v2
1–2 word phrases: speaking_rate 0.85, noise_scale 0.4. Longer phrases: noise_scale 0.5. All 1,310
clips were regenerated, and all pass QA. In a 12-word stress test the mean similarity went from
0.15 to 0.29.
On the same 40 sampled clips: 1–2 words 0.44 → 0.45, 3+ words 0.84 → 0.86.

## Conclusion
Sentence audio is fine for learning. Single-word audio is the weakest part of the product. The real
fixes are (1) Family voice recordings, which already override TTS, and (2) Azure ur-PK neural voices
before any paid tier (SPEC §9). Slow mode and the Nastaliq/Roman text cover the gap meanwhile.

## Change: voice v3 (G12)
1–2 word phrases are synthesised inside a carrier sentence ("یہ لفظ X ہے") and cut back out using
MMS's own per-character durations, captured with a hook on the duration predictor. On the same
12-word stress test, Whisper similarity went from **0.23 to 0.70** (بات، دل، بارات، چار and روٹی now
round-trip perfectly). Longer phrases are synthesised directly with noise_scale 0.5.
