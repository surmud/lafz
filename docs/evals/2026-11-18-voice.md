# Voice trial: Matcha-TTS Urdu vs Meta MMS (H13)

Method: 60 random course phrases (19 of 1–2 words, 41 sentences), each synthesised by every voice and
transcribed by Whisper large-v3-turbo (MLX). Score = 1 − edit distance / length on normalised Urdu
(the same metric as `scripts/audio_audit.py`). Script: `scripts/voice_trial.py`.

| voice | licence | words | sentences |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Meta MMS (`mms-urd-3`, carrier-sentence cut) | CC-BY-NC 4.0 | 0.748 | 0.837 |
| Matcha-TTS Urdu female + BSC Vocos | Apache-2.0 | 0.787 | 0.925 |
| Matcha-TTS Urdu male + BSC Vocos | Apache-2.0 | 0.750 | 0.941 |

Short-word tuning (19 words): female plain 0.755 → length 1.2 **0.824**; male plain 0.696 → full stop
+ length 1.15 **0.829**.

Decision: switch to Matcha (voice v4). Two voices: male characters (Bilal, Abbu, Pervez, Bashir) get
the male voice; everything else the female one. Generator also re-synthesises any take Whisper scores
below 0.9 (sentences) / 0.8 (words), keeping the best of 4. Full run: 1,940 clips, mean round-trip
**0.942**, 0 QA failures. Bonus: the licence is now Apache-2.0 instead of non-commercial.

Weakest clips (< 0.6), all very short words where Whisper itself guesses: ہاں, چچی, صبح, چھ, ہفتہ,
پرسوں, پھول, مسجد, پانچ سو, غزل, گل. Worth a human listen (G15-style), not blocking.

Candidates not tried: Indic Parler-TTS (gated), Orpheus Urdu fine-tunes (3B LLM, heavy, quality
unverified), OmniVoice Urdu (voice cloning; promising, next to try), F5-TTS OpenBible Urdu
(CC-BY-SA, needs a reference clip).
