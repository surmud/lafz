# Brand guardrails

Lafz must never be mistaken for Duolingo (or any other app).

- **No bird or owl mascot.** The cast is the family: Khala, Nani, Bilal, Ammi, Abbu, Mrs. Butt, Pervez, Bashir Bhai. Original art only.
- **Palette:** teal `#0f7a73` (primary), amber `#f0a315`, rose `#b8325a`, cream background `#fbf4e8`, plus the truck-art stripe. Never Duolingo's lime green (`#58cc02`) as a brand colour; the green used for correct answers is a muted `#257a3a`.
- **Type:** Fraunces for display, the platform's plain system font for body, Noto Nastaliq Urdu for Urdu. No rounded "friendly" sans (Feather, DIN Round, SF Pro Rounded, Nunito) as the brand face.
- **Words:** XP, streak (which rests, never resets), can-dos, levels named for family roles. Never hearts, gems, lingots, energy, leagues or "owl". The one exception is the joke that there are no hearts.
- **Tone:** warm and funny about family life; plain labels in menus and onboarding; never guilt, never nagging.
- **Poetry:** public-domain poets only (Ghalib, Mir, Iqbal and older). No Faiz, Faraz, Parveen Shakir, Jaun Elia or other in-copyright poets.
